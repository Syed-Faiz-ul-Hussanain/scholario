About Us

Scholarship access for every student

We built ScholarMatched to level the playing field; giving every ambitious student the same quality of guidance that used to belong only to those with the right connections.

What we do

ScholarMatched is an AI-powered platform that matches students with scholarships they are actually eligible for, based on their nationality, degree level, academic background, field of study, and goals. No more scrolling through hundreds of irrelevant listings.

We surface the right opportunities, help students prepare their applications, and guide them through every step, from writing their Statement of Purpose to preparing for a scholarship interview.

Why we built this

The scholarship application process is broken. Talented students from Ethiopia, Yamen, Nigeria, Indonesia, Pakistan, and dozens of other countries spend months applying to scholarships they never had a real chance of winning, because they didn't know which ones fit their profile, or how to present themselves compellingly.

At the same time, students from better-connected backgrounds had access to private counsellors, alumni networks, and insider advice that made the process far less mysterious. That gap is not about merit. It's about access.

ScholarMatched was built to close that gap; permanently, and at scale. We believe that a student in Addis Ababa, Aden or Lagos deserves the same quality of scholarship guidance as one at a well-connected university in London or New York.
