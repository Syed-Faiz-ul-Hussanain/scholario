ScholarMatched provides a complete scholarship management system, not just a scholarship database. From discovering scholarships that match your profile, tracking applications and deadlines, managing documents, storing motivation letters, reviewing SOPs, prepartion blog, and preparing for interviews, everything is organized in one place. Instead of juggling spreadsheets, bookmarks, PDFs, and multiple websites, ScholarMatched helps you manage the entire journey from scholarship search to acceptance.

Our AI analyses your profile across 20+ dimensions degree level, CGPA, nationality, English scores, and more then scores every scholarship in our database to surface the ones where you have the highest chance of winning.

Yes. The Application Tracker lets you move scholarships through stages: Saved → Applying → Submitted → Shortlisted → Accepted or Rejected. You also get email reminders 3 days before each deadline so nothing slips through.

The SOP Reviewer is an AI-powered tool that reads your Statement of Purpose or motivation letter and gives you a structured score across key dimensions clarity, relevance, motivation, and fit. Scores are ranked against the specific goals and priorities of the scholarship you select, so feedback is always tailored to what that programme is actually looking for. It highlights strengths and weaknesses without rewriting your text, so the voice stays yours.

Go to the SOP Reviewer from your dashboard, select the scholarship you're applying to, then either paste your letter directly or upload a PDF or TXT file. The AI analyses it in seconds and returns a detailed breakdown with a score and actionable feedback.

Yes. Your last uploaded or pasted letter is saved so you can pick up where you left off, run a new review after edits, or compare the same letter across different scholarships and programmes. We added this feature because many students apply to dozens of scholarships and later receive an interview invitation. In those situations, reviewing the exact motivation letter you submitted is extremely important for preparation, but finding that specific version among many applications can be time-consuming. ScholarMatched keeps your letters organized and accessible whenever you need them.
